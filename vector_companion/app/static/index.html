Jarvis

Just talk to him by name, in one breath: "Jarvis, what's the weather tomorrow?", "Jarvis, play blackjack", "What time is it, Jarvis?". Say only "Jarvis" and you get a chime and his listening face; then just say what you want. Keep talking for follow-ups; "that's all" ends it, "stop listening" turns the mic off. Everything he hears is processed on this Mac and only acted on when you say his name.
